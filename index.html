Chasing Water

An August guide to finding water in San Benito County — from a reservoir hidden among the Pinnacles to places where you can actually jump in and cool off.

August in inland San Benito County can be extremely hot. Check current hours, closures, weather, fire conditions and facility schedules before heading out. At Pinnacles, carry plenty of water, avoid peak afternoon heat, and remember that swimming or bathing in natural park waters is prohibited.
